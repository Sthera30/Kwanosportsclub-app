# Kwano Sports Club - Community Sports Fundraising Website

## About the Project

Kwano Sports Club is a website i developed for a client who wanted to raise funds and collect sports equipment to support the development of tennis courts within their community.

The project was inspired by a desire to create positive opportunities for young people. With crime among young people being a concern in the community, the client's vision is to encourage youth participation in sports by providing them with a constructive and meaningful way to spend their time.
